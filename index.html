Browser Space God Prototype

Pairwise Newtonian gravity, leapfrog stepping, density-based masses, and energy-threshold impacts.

Create: left drag empty space. Select: left click body.

  • Wheel: zoom
  • Middle/right drag: pan
  • WASD/arrows: pan
  • Space: pause
  • F: follow selected
  • O: cycle type
  • Tab: cycle material
  • 1-4: time scale
  • [ / ]: slower/faster
  • 5: impact demo
  • R: reset camera
  • Esc: clear selection
